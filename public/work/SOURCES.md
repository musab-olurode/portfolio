Scrapays reel (scrapays-reel.webm / .mp4 / -poster.webp): built the same way from a Playwright recording of https://scrapays.com/ and Google Play listing screenshots 2 and 4 of Scrapays Agent (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scrapays.agent), which show the app as it is today.
